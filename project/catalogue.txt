Implementation notes for Claude
Use these four consumer-facing website categories: Electric Scooters, Lithium Batteries, Inverter & Battery, Solar Solutions.
Do not rename UKAVA model names.
Do not add range, charging time, price, bestseller/quick-delivery claims, battery capacity, or other specifications unless they appear in this reference for that product.
For homepage cards, show only a small subset of catalogue-supported specifications; keep the complete set for product-detail pages.
Where a catalogue says On Demand, Available, Yes, Without, or uses unusual wording, preserve that meaning rather than inferring a different specification.
Use product-detail pages for full specifications, image galleries/colour variants when assets exist, enquiry/dealer CTAs, and related products from the same website category.
