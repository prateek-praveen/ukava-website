Energy + Electric Mobility

Building India's energy and mobility ecosystem.

UKAVA manufactures lithium-powered products across electric scooters, lithium batteries, inverter systems and solar solutions — engineered under one brand for homes, businesses and roads.

What UKAVA builds

Electric Scooters

Nine models across scooters, three-wheelers and cargo vehicles.

Lithium Batteries

LFP batteries for inverters, two- and three-wheelers, and energy storage.

Inverter & Battery

LINVA inverters with inbuilt lithium batteries for homes, offices and shops.

Solar Solutions

LINVASOL solar PCUs with inbuilt lithium batteries and panel sizing.

Engineered in India, built to last

Manufactured in India

Built domestically with UKAVA's own engineering and quality control.

LFP battery expertise

Lithium chemistry with built-in battery management, applied across energy and mobility.

One connected portfolio

Mobility, batteries, backup and solar engineered under a single brand.

Warranty on every product

Up to 10 years on lithium inverter batteries and LINVA systems; 3 years across vehicle battery ranges.

For Businesses

Grow with UKAVA across energy & electric mobility.

Dealers and distributors can work across the full portfolio — scooters, lithium batteries, LINVA inverters and LINVASOL solar systems.
